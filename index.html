Kingsmere

A Home Designed to Stand Apart.

Introducing Kingsmere, one of Asia Crown's distinguished home offerings, designed for families who want a home that feels modern, comfortable and truly their own.

With a 73 sq.m. floor area on a 150 sq.m. lot, Kingsmere is thoughtfully planned across two floors to make everyday living comfortable and practical.

The ground floor features a welcoming living area, dining area, kitchen, open lanai, and common bathroom, along with a one-car carport, pathway, and mini landscape area. It is a layout made for both everyday routines and meaningful moments with family and friends.

Upstairs, the home offers a master bedroom, two additional bedrooms, a common bathroom, hallway, and balcony, giving each member of the family a comfortable space to rest, recharge, and enjoy a little privacy.

Choose the look that feels like you. Kingsmere is available in three carefully selected color palettes, giving homeowners the freedom to choose the character they want for their home: PEARLSTONE, DARKSTONE and BLOOMSTONE.

At Asia Crown Corporation, we believe a home should offer more than just a place to live. It should be a place where families grow, celebrations happen, and memories are made.

Kingsmere is our offering for those who want comfort, style and value ... all in one home.

Kingswell

The space your family deserves.

Meet Kingswell, a thoughtfully designed two-storey home made for families who want more room to live, grow and create memories together.

With a generous 95 sq.m. floor area on a 150 sq.m. lot, Kingswell gives you the extra space you need without losing the comfort and practicality of a well-planned home.

The ground floor brings everyone together with a welcoming living area, dining area, kitchen, open lanai, and common bathroom. It also includes Bedroom 1, giving you the flexibility to accommodate a family member, guest, or even create a space that suits your changing needs. A one-car carport, pathway and mini landscape area complete the ground floor layout.

Upstairs is where the family’s private spaces come together. Kingswell features a master bedroom, two additional bedrooms, a common bathroom, hallway and balcony. With three bedrooms on the second floor, there is plenty of room for everyone to have a space of their own.

Kingswell is available in three distinctive color palettes, so you can choose the look that best reflects your personality: DARKSTONE, PEARLSTONE and BLOOMSTONE.

Whether you prefer a stronger modern character, a classic and sophisticated look, or a warmer and more welcoming feel, Kingswell gives you three beautiful ways to make the house feel like your own.

At Asia Crown Corporation, we believe that a good home should fit the way families actually live. Kingswell gives you the space to welcome guests, enjoy family meals, have quiet moments of your own and grow into the home through the years.

Kingswell is not simply a bigger house. It is more room for the life you’re building.

Kingsley

A home that rises above the ordinary

There’s something special about coming home to a space that feels like it was made with your family in mind.

Kingsley is a 115 sq.m. two-storey home on a 150 sq.m. lot, designed with generous spaces, thoughtful details and a layout that gives every part of the family home its purpose.

The ground floor brings together the spaces where life happens, an inviting living area, dining area, kitchen, open lanai, common bathroom, and a bedroom. The one-car carport, pathway, and mini landscape area complete the front of the home, giving it both practicality and a welcoming touch.

But Kingsley truly reveals its character upstairs. The master bedroom comes with its own bathroom and walk-in closet, creating a private retreat within the home. Two more bedrooms share the second floor, together with a common bathroom and a balcony. The second-floor layout also features an open-to-below space, adding a sense of openness and giving the interior a distinctive character.

Kingsley comes in three distinctive color palettes: DARKSTONE, PEARLSTONE and BLOOMSTONE.

Each palette gives Kingsley a different personality, while keeping the same thoughtful home design at its heart.

Kingsley is for the family that wants a home with a little more distinction and a place that feels personal, comfortable and ready for the years ahead.

This is not just about having a bigger space. It’s about coming home to something that feels truly yours.

Kingsford

A home built for the way families live

Some homes are made for starting out. Kingsford is made for settling in, growing together and enjoying more of what home has to offer.

With a 139 sq.m. floor area on a spacious 300 sq.m. lot, Kingsford gives families the room to live comfortably today while having space for the years ahead.

From the moment you enter, the home feels ready for family life. The ground floor brings together a living area, dining area, kitchen, open lanai, common bathroom, and two bedrooms. It also features a two-car carport, giving you the convenience of having enough parking space for the family.

Upstairs, Kingsford becomes even more personal. The master bedroom comes with its own bathroom and walk-in closet, while two additional bedrooms provide comfortable private spaces for family members. A common bathroom, hallway, and balcony complete the second floor.

With four bedrooms in total, generous common areas, two-car parking, and a larger lot, Kingsford is designed for families who appreciate having room for everyone and room for the things that matter.

Kingsford is available in three color palettes, each giving the home its own character: DARKSTONE, PEARLSTONE and BLOOMSTONE. Whichever palette you choose, Kingsford remains a home that makes a statement without trying too hard.

At Asia Crown Corporation, we want every home we offer to feel like a place you can truly grow into. Kingsford gives you the space, privacy and comfort to build the kind of family life you’ve always imagined.

Because sometimes, a home isn't just about having enough space. It's about having space for everything that comes with a life well lived.

Kingston

Made for a life that keeps growing.

Some families need a little more room. Others need a home that can keep up with everything life brings. Kingston was designed for both. With a generous 170 sq.m. floor area on a 300 sq.m. lot, Kingston is the largest of the home models in this collection, giving families the freedom to enjoy more space without compromising the warmth of a family home.

The ground floor is made for everyday living and entertaining. It features a living area, dining area, kitchen, open lanai, common bathroom, and two bedrooms, giving you the flexibility to accommodate family members or guests comfortably. A two-car carport, pathway, and mini landscape area add convenience from the moment you arrive home.

Upstairs is where Kingston really sets itself apart. The second floor offers a master bedroom with its own bathroom and walk-in closet, plus three additional bedrooms. One of the bedrooms also comes with its own walk-in closet, giving the family even more storage and personal space.

With five bedrooms, multiple bathrooms, generous living spaces, two-car parking, and a spacious lot, Kingston is built for families whose lives are always moving—children growing up, relatives visiting, celebrations happening, and new memories being made.

Every family has its own style, so Kingston comes in three color palettes: BLOOMSTONE, PEARLSTONE and DARKSTONE. Different looks, different personalities, one Kingston designed around the way your family lives.

Kingston isn't trying to be the biggest house on the street. It simply gives you the kind of space that makes life easier, more bedrooms, more privacy, more places to gather and more room for whatever comes next.

Because when your family grows, your home should be ready to grow with you.
